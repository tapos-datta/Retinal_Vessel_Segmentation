Columns: RGB input, reference segmentation, then the predictions of rgb, hybrid and hybrid_weber. In the prediction panels green = true positive, red = false positive, blue = false negative, over a faint grey copy of the image; only pixels inside the FOV are coloured, because only those are scored.
Rows: CHASE_DB1 Image_14L, HRF 15_dr and FIVES_test_101_G. CHASE_DB1 and HRF are predicted by the seed-1 LODO model that held that dataset out; FIVES by the seed-1 model trained on all 113 pooled images. These images were fixed in advance as the showcase images of the enhancement gallery (visualize_enhancement.py --final), not chosen after seeing these predictions. They were chosen to show the enhancement's appearance and are not typical of the Dice means; see figures/predictions/typical/ for images chosen to be typical.
Predictions as in evaluate.py: 8-fold TTA, threshold 0.5, at original resolution, Dice inside the FOV. Dice per panel: CHASE_DB1 Image_14L: rgb 0.820, hybrid 0.817, hybrid_weber 0.794; HRF 15_dr: rgb 0.680, hybrid 0.758, hybrid_weber 0.708; FIVES FIVES_test_101_G: rgb 0.701, hybrid 0.469, hybrid_weber 0.525.
