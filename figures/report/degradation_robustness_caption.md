Mean Dice per degradation level for frozen LODO models on their own 113 held-out images; line = mean over seeds 1–3 of the per-seed mean, shading = range of the three seed means. Lines join levels within one family (tone with a bright optic disc, haze lowering vessel contrast, noise, and the matched chains). No TTA in the degradation test, threshold 0.5, Dice inside the FOV at original resolution.
Levels are calibrated per image: each step moves a property of that image by the factor separating the darkest FIVES quarter from the training images, and `fives` combines them (see the Degradation test section of REPORT.md); 52 images reach the `fives` targets only partly. Shaded columns: disc_32 and fives, the levels used by the stage 6 and 6b robustness rules. Right: one image at original, disc_32 and fives.
At the FIVES-matched level `fives` the order is hybrid_weber > hybrid > enh3 > clahe > rgb; on FIVES itself (3-seed means, 792 images) it is hybrid_weber > rgb > clahe > enh3 > hybrid (rgb and clahe tie at 4 decimals). Among rgb, clahe, hybrid and enh3 the ranking here is the reverse of the FIVES ranking, so the degradation does not reproduce what goes wrong on FIVES; hybrid_weber is first in both.
Data: results/degradation (rgb, clahe, hybrid, enh3) and results/degradation_stage6b (hybrid_weber), 3 seeds each; all variants present at every level.
