Background-intensity distribution inside the FOV (reference label = 0) per dataset, for the green channel, CLAHE on the green channel, and the output of the hybrid and hybrid_weber enhancement front-ends run on their own over the whole image at original resolution. Images: CHASE_DB1 28, DRIVE 20, HRF 45, STARE 20, FIVES 792 (FIVES: the images with a non-blank label; 2 blank-label images left out). FIVES is dashed.
Checkpoints (seed 1; each dataset's images go through the model that never saw that dataset in training, FIVES through the model trained on all 113 pooled images): CHASE_DB1 runs/{hybrid,hybrid_weber}/lodo/lodo_CHASE_DB1/seed1/best.pt; DRIVE runs/{hybrid,hybrid_weber}/lodo/lodo_DRIVE/seed1/best.pt; HRF runs/{hybrid,hybrid_weber}/lodo/lodo_HRF/seed1/best.pt; STARE runs/{hybrid,hybrid_weber}/lodo/lodo_STARE/seed1/best.pt; FIVES runs/{hybrid,hybrid_weber}/all/all/seed1/best.pt. Green and CLAHE do not depend on a model.
Each panel has its own x-axis. The green and CLAHE panels are 8-bit intensities in [0, 1]; the hybrid and hybrid_weber panels are in each module's own output units (its own grey range), so positions cannot be compared across panels, only the spread between curves within a panel.
Dataset gap (as in visualize_enhancement.py): per image the background median, per dataset the median of those; gap = mean absolute difference between dataset medians over all pairs, divided by the IQR of 4000 random FOV pixels per image. '4 training sets' uses CHASE_DB1, DRIVE, HRF and STARE only (pairs and IQR), the existing definition; 'with FIVES' adds FIVES to the pairs and to the IQR. With 4000 pixels per image, the FIVES images (792 of 905) dominate the IQR of the five-dataset gap.
